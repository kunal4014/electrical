Common questions

Before you get in touch.

Quick answers about the services and enquiry process.

What electrical work can I enquire about?+

Lighting, TV & data, switchboard upgrades, sheds & pergolas, power points and general residential electrical work are all listed services.

Do you work on outdoor areas?+

Yes — the listed services include shed and pergola lighting, power, ceiling fans and other practical outdoor electrical solutions.

How do I check whether my suburb is covered?+

Use the service-area checker above. It will carry your suburb or postcode into the enquiry so you can confirm coverage directly.

What if the issue is urgent?+

Use the Emergency Call Out option or call directly on 0426 884 622 to discuss availability for an urgent electrical fault or safety concern.

Can I send photos of the job?+

Yes. The quick quote prepares an SMS; once Messages opens, attach any helpful photos before sending it.
